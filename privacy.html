Privacy Policy

Vault Dominion · Last updated: August 9, 2026

Short version: Vault Dominion collects no personal data. The game makes no external API calls, has no account or login system, and does not send any information off your device.

What the game stores

Vault Dominion saves a single piece of information — your best level reached — using your browser or device's local storage (localStorage). This value:

  • Stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Is not linked to your identity, an account, or any other person.
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's storage/data or uninstalling the app.

What the game does not do

  • No account creation, login, or authentication of any kind.
  • No collection of names, emails, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • No location, camera, microphone, or sensor access.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No network requests to any server, API, or third party while playing.

Permissions

Vault Dominion does not request any device permissions beyond what is needed to display and run the game locally (e.g. installing it to your home screen as a Progressive Web App).

Children's privacy

Because the game collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this game's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
